New in Corpus Christi · inside Echelon Performance Gym

CoPa Café

A specialty micro cafe hiding where the gym is — in the London District area of Corpus Christi. Espresso, matcha and chai, each built around a gourmet flavor combination — with plain cold brew for the purists. The fall menu is out now.

How the counter works

Two choices. That’s the whole order.

1

Pick a base

Espresso, matcha or chai. Cold brew stands on its own, no ceremony required.

2

Pick a pairing

Blueberry & cardamom. Fig & brown sugar. Pumpkin & cookie butter — and more pairings on the board.

3

Drink it at the gym

You’re already at the entrance. The counter is steps from the gym floor.
