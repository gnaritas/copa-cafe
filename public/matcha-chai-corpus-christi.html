Tea & cold brew · Corpus Christi

Matcha, chai & cold brew

“The drinks focus on espresso, matcha and chai” — what The Bend Magazine saw on the fall menu. Tea here is a base, not a consolation prize. And cold brew stays exactly itself.

Matcha

Ground green tea, whisked into the menu’s center

Matcha is the whole leaf, stone-ground and whisked, so it carries the green-tea bitterness with it rather than drowning it in milk. On this board it can also wear a pairing — blueberry-cardamom reads differently, and better, on a grassy base.

Chai

Spiced tea, built for a pairing too

Chai’s own spice work — cardamom up front, warm layers behind — makes it the base that argues least with the flavor combinations. Fig and brown sugar on chai is a quiet winter-drink move.

Cold brew

Slow-steeped coffee · no fluff

“Fear not, cold brew lovers without the fluff; they’ve got that, too” — the magazine’s line says it plainly. Slow-steeped, served as coffee: no pairing required, no syrup pilgrimage, in and out before leg day.
