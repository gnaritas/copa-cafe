The fall board

The fall menu

The counter works in two moves: choose your caffeine base, then pick a gourmet flavor combination. Bases first, then the pairings — and plain cold brew for everyone who wants none of the rest.

Step one · the bases

Choose your base

  • Espresso — the bar’s anchor; the pairing program is built around it.
  • Matcha — ground green tea, a full base, not an afterthought.
  • Chai — spiced tea, ready for a pairing of its own.
  • Cold brew — slow-steeped coffee, straight up, no fluff.

Matcha, chai & cold brew page

Step two · the pairings

Pick your flavor combination

  • Blueberry & cardamom — deep fruit against cool, resinous spice.
  • Fig & brown sugar — jammy fig, molasses-deep sugar.
  • Pumpkin & cookie butter — fall done as spiced-cookie richness, not pumpkin-spice rerun.
  • And more — the board carries combinations beyond the ones printed here.
