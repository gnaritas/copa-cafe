Espresso · Corpus Christi

The espresso bar & its pairings

Most cafes ask what size you want. CoPa’s counter asks two real questions: what base, and what pairing. Espresso is where the pairing program starts.

Blueberry & cardamom

Deep fruit · cool spice

Blueberry is fruit with weight and a bass note — nothing like the syrups on the flavored-coffee rack. Cardamom is cool, faintly eucalyptus, a spice that cuts rather than coats. Together they read like the dessert-plate pairing they are: the bright and the resinous, in the same cup.

Fig & brown sugar

Jammy fruit · molasses depth

Fig brings jam and a hint of seedy texture; brown sugar brings the molasses roundness that dark-roast espresso is already reaching for. This is the subtle one of the fall board — it tastes like it belongs to whoever ordered it.

Pumpkin & cookie butter

Squash sweetness · spiced-cookie richness

Every cafe does pumpkin this time of year. CoPa does pumpkin paired with cookie butter — the caramelized, spiced-cookie spread — so the fall drink lands closer to a cookie at the bottom of the cup than to a spice jar.

More than the three

“And more” is part of the menu

The Bend Magazine’s fall-menu report lists these three pairings and then — correctly — “and more.” The board at the counter is the live list; this page keeps the named ones. The pairings work on the cafe’s bases: espresso, matcha or chai. Cold brew stays unbothered.
