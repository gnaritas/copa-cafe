Privacy

What this site does with your information

The contact form

The form asks for a name and a message, and optionally a phone number and an email address. Those details go to CoPa Café so the counter can answer you — nothing else. Notes are kept only as long as it takes to reply and settle whatever you asked about.

No cookies, no trackers

This site sets no cookies, runs no advertising pixels and loads no third-party scripts. There is no analytics software watching your visit and nothing to opt out of.

The map

The Visit page embeds a Google Maps view. When that map loads, Google receives the standard request data of your browser (including its IP address), under Google’s own privacy terms. Every other page loads without contacting Google.

Questions about your note

Ask the cafe through the contact form to see, correct or delete what a note contains. We will honor it.
